HUONG DAN: "VE DONG THUAN" (consensus) LA GI VA HOAT DONG THE NAO
=================================================================
Doc file nay truoc khi sua/chay bat ky gi lien quan den predict_next_draw_consensus.py,
predict/consensus/, predict/history/*_consensus.txt hoac dashboard (docs/).

0. TOM TAT 5 DONG
-----------------
- Moi "model" = 1 dai seed lien tiep (bat dau tu seed_start). Moi (seed, ky) cho ra DUNG 1 ve bang 1 ham hash
  co dinh (muc 2). Khong co "AI" hay hoc may nao trong cong thuc nay.
- Seed "trung" 1 ky = seed do cho ra dung ve trung that (5 so + so dac biet) cua ky do. Cac seed trung duoc luu o l1_merged/.
- VE DONG THUAN cho 1 ky dang xet: nhieu seed KHAC NHAU, moi seed tung trung o 1 ky KHAC, khi cung ap len ky dang xet
  thi cho ra CUNG 1 ve. MUC DONG THUAN cua ve = so seed doc lap cung ra ve do (muc 3 = 3 seed, muc 4 = 4 seed...).
- Ban zip nay chi giu 3 model (682305800400, 108477750639, 1903987714639) va chi xuat du doan MUC 3 va MUC 4.
- QUAN TRONG: cac backtest da chay CHUA CHUNG MINH ve dong thuan tot hon boc so ngau nhien (xem muc 7). Day la co che
  dang thu nghiem/doi chieu, khong phai tin hieu du doan da duoc kiem chung.

1. THUAT NGU
------------
ky / draw_id     So thu tu ky quay (vi du 905). Ket qua that nam o data/all.csv (cot draw_id, result_json).
ve               5 so chinh trong 1..35 (khong lap, khong quan tam thu tu) + 1 so dac biet 1..12.
                 So ve co the co = C(35,5) x 12 = 324.632 x 12 = 3.895.584. Xac suat 1 ve ngau nhien trung ~ 1/3.895.584.
seed             So nguyen 64-bit dung lam dau vao cua ham hash. Cung 1 seed o ky khac nhau cho ve khac nhau.
model            Dai seed [seed_start, seed_end]. Dinh danh model = seed_start (vd 682305800400). Moi model co 1 file
                 l1_merged/merged_seed{seed_start}.json. CAU HINH HIEN TAI (workflow): 682305800400 quet 10 ty seed
                 (~2.570 seed trung/ky); 108477750639 va 1903987714639 quet ~31 ty seed (20 chunk, ~8.000 seed trung/ky).
                 DU LIEU l1_merged TRONG ZIP NAY CON LA BAN QUET CU (682305800400: ~1,56 ty seed, ~400 seed/ky; cac model
                 khac 60 ky) cho toi khi workflow chay lai - cac con so "~400 seed/ky" o muc 7 la cua ban quet cu.
J1               "Jackpot 1" = seed cho ra DUNG ve trung that (5 so + dac biet) o 1 ky cu the.
L1               Tap seed J1: l1_merged/merged_seed*.json -> draws[].seeds la cac seed trung ky draws[].draw_id.
L2               l2_merged/promoted_seed{seed_start}.json (do check_l1_merged.py tao; ban zip nay khong kem file L2 nao):
                 seed trung >= 2 ky, duoc "thang hang" khoi L1. L2 LUU SEED TRUNG THEO KY o 2 chieu:
                   "promoted": [{"seed","j1_count","hits":[{"draw_id","draw_date"},...]}]   seed -> cac ky no trung
                   "draws":    [{"draw_id","draw_date","found","seeds":[...]}]              ky -> cac seed L2 trung ky do
                 Khi tinh dong thuan, L1 + L2 duoc gop va KHU TRUNG theo seed (seed L2 da bi xoa khoi L1 nen PHAI gop).
ky nguon         Ky ma seed da tung trung (o l1_merged). "Khac ky" o dinh nghia dong thuan nghia la ky nguon != ky dang xet.
pool             Tap seed dua vao de tinh dong thuan = cac seed da tung trung (o bat ky ky nao) cua model, da khu trung.

2. CACH SINH VE TU (seed, ky)  -- lotto_common.predict_ticket()
----------------------------------------------------------------
    M1 = 0x9E3779B97F4A7C15   M2 = 0xD1B54A32D192ED03   M3 = 0xBF58476D1CE4E5B9   M4 = 0x94D049BB133111EB
    combined = (seed * M1 + draw_id * M2) mod 2^64
    mixed    = mix64(combined)                    # splitmix64: x^=x>>30; x*=M3; x^=x>>27; x*=M4; x^=x>>31
    rank     = mixed mod 324632                   # chi so to hop C(35,5) -> tap 5 so (build_rank_to_mask)
    special  = (mix64(mixed) mod 12) + 1
Cong thuc giong scan_v2.cpp / scan_per_draw.cpp / TicketFormula.dart. Vector hoa bang numpy o predict_next_draw_consensus.py
(mix64_vec). "Khoa ve" dung trong code: key = rank*12 + (special-1)  (0 .. 3.895.583).
Dac tinh: (seed, ky) -> ve la ham xac dinh, phan bo gan deu tren 3.895.584 ve. Vi vay cac seed "roi" vao 1 ve nao do
o 1 ky la ngau nhien; so seed cung roi vao 1 ve tuan theo phan phoi gan Poisson.

3. VE DONG THUAN - DINH NGHIA CHINH XAC
---------------------------------------
Voi model M va ky dang xet D (D co the la ky SAP TOI chua co ket qua, hoac 1 ky da qua de kiem chung):
  1) pool(M, D) = tat ca seed thuoc M da tung trung o BAT KY ky nao KHAC D (o du doan that: moi ky da co ket qua).
     Khu trung: 1 seed trung nhieu ky van chi la 1 seed.
  2) Voi moi seed trong pool tinh ve(seed, D) theo muc 2.
  3) Gom theo ve. MUC DONG THUAN cua 1 ve = so seed DOC LAP (khac nhau) cho ra ve do. Ve co muc >= 2 goi la "ve dong thuan".
  4) Neu 1 seed L2 trung 2 ky thi van chi tinh 1 seed (khong phai 2 phieu). Code dem theo seed da khu trung, KHONG dem
     theo so dong (xem docstring find_ticket_at_level()).
Y nghia: cac seed nay den tu cac ky nguon khac nhau, moi seed la 1 "ung vien" da tung dung o 1 ky khac; neu chung cung
chi vao 1 ve o ky D thi noi la "dong thuan". Muc cang cao thi ve cang hiem trong 3,9 trieu ve.

Vi du that (ky 905, model 682305800400, muc 3), co the tu kiem chung:
    ve 02-04-15-25-32 + DB 06
      seed 682386253667 (da trung ky 545), seed 682716373322 (ky 419), seed 682751405342 (ky 440)
    -> ve(682386253667, 905) = ve(682716373322, 905) = ve(682751405342, 905) = 02-04-15-25-32 + DB 06.
Kiem chung: python3 explain_consensus_ticket.py 682305800400 905 --levels 3,4

Ve TRUNG va KHONG TRUNG deu co the dong thuan: "trung" chi nghia la ve do trung khop voi ket qua that cua ky D
(khi ky D da co ket qua). Muc dong thuan cua ve trung = so seed tu ky khac cung sinh ra ve trung do (thuong 0, 1 hoac 2).
Chay them: python3 explain_consensus_ticket.py 682305800400 514 --levels 2 --show-actual

4. CACH CHON VE DU DOAN  -- predict_next_draw_consensus.py
----------------------------------------------------------
  - Ky muc tieu = ky sap toi (get_next_draw_id tren data/all.csv). Doc L1 (+L2 neu co) cua tung model.
  - Model co < MIN_MODEL_SEEDS (mac dinh 100.000) seed thi BO QUA.
  - Moi muc trong PREFERRED_LEVELS (ban nay mac dinh "3,4"; ban goc "2,3,4,5,6,7") ra 1 ve RIENG cho model do.
  - Voi moi muc: lay cac ve co DUNG muc do; boc ngau nhien co hat co dinh random.Random(f"{ky}:{seed_start}:consensus")
    toi da TICKETS_PER_LEVEL (mac dinh 2) ve khac nhau, CHI GIU ve cuoi cung. Neu khong co ve nao dung muc thi lay ve co
    muc GAN nhat (uu tien cao hon) va ghi ro "KHONG co ve nao dung ...".
  - seed dai dien cua ve = seed nho nhat trong nhom seed dong thuan cua ve do.
  - Ket qua: predict/consensus/next_draw_predict_{seed_start}.txt (1 file/model, kem danh sach seed + ky da trung),
    predict/next_draw_predict_consensus.txt (tong hop), predict/history/{ky:05d}_consensus.txt (de doi chieu / dashboard).

6. PHAN BIET 2 CACH TINH (de khoi nham khi doc ket qua phan tich)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  a) Du doan that (san xuat): pool = seed cua cac ky DA CO (truoc ky sap toi). Khong nhin thay tuong lai.
  b) Phan tich "ky khac" (leave-one-out): voi ky D da qua, pool = seed cua MOI ky khac D (ca truoc lan sau D), de xem ve trung
     that co muc dong thuan ra sao. Chi la phan tich mo ta, khong dung de du doan. explain_consensus_ticket.py dung cach (b);
     voi ky sap toi (chua co ket qua) hai cach trung nhau.
  c) Cach walk-forward (pool chi gom seed cua ky < D) cung da duoc thu - cho ket luan tuong tu.

7. KET QUA DA DO (model 682305800400, 904 ky, cach b) - DOC KY TRUOC KHI KY VONG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Ky vong ngau nhien ~0,093 seed/ky (361.460 luot trung / 3.895.584 ve).

    KHONG co ky nao ve muc cao nhat lai la ve trung that (0/904).
  - So seed trung dung 2 ky: 59 (ky vong ngau nhien ~42, lech ~2,6 sigma, chua hieu chinh da phep thu -> khong ket luan duoc).
  - Cac model 108477750639 / 1903987714639 (cua so 60 ky): ve trung khong bao gio dat muc >= 4; 1903987714639 co ky 883 dat muc 2.
  - backtest_consensus_batch.py / backtest_predictions.py (846 ky): trung binh so trung cua ve dong thuan cao nhat ~0,70/5,
    khong cao hon moc ngau nhien 0,7143/5; khong chien luoc nao co y nghia thong ke sau hieu chinh Bonferroni.
  KET LUAN THAN TRONG: muc dong thuan khong (chua) phan biet duoc ve trung voi ve khong trung. Xem nhu so lieu doi chieu.

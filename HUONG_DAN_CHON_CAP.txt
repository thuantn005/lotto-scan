CHON CAP (COT LOI) - TAI HET CAP -> NGHIEN CUU/BACKTEST -> MOI CHON CAP
=====================================================================
Cot loi la CAP, khong phai ve con lai. Cap "trung" = cap co seed sinh dung ve that cua ky do -> ve that bi loai oan -> ve con lai VO NGHIA.
Mot bo cap THANH CONG khi KHONG cap nao trung. Xac suat thanh cong ~ (so ve con lai) / (to hop): cang chon nhieu cap, ve con lai cang it nhung xac suat thanh cong cang thap.

QUY TRINH moi ky (chon_cap_release.py, goi tu scan_v2_auto.yml cho Lotto va Mega):
 1. TAI HET moi cap tren Release (khong chi cap duoc chon -> het thien lech chon tap). Cap nao xu ly xong xoa gz ngay.
    Cap nhat lich su TAT CA cap: chon_cap/cap_lich_su_<game>.txt (cap | so seed | cac ky cap da trung | den ky | tu ky), cua so HIST=120 ky.
 2. NGHIEN CUU + BACKTEST MU (nghien_cuu_chon_cap.py) tren lich su TOAN BO cap -> bao_cao/nghien_cuu_chon_cap_<game>.txt
    - So cac quy tac xep hang: ngau nhien (doi chung), kich thuoc cap, lich su (K=20, K=5), gan day (10 ky).
    - Ky D chi dung lich su ky < D. Nua dau = train, nua sau = test. Do bang z (hypergeometric, cung tap cap tung ky).
    - CHI duyet quy tac neu z_test <= -2.5 va z_train <= -1.0 va >= 15 ky test. Khong quy tac nao dat -> chon theo ly thuyet (it seed nhat) + hoa ngau nhien,
      va ghi ro "chua hon ngau nhien".
 3. MOI CHON CAP: chon_cap/chon_cap_<game>_<ky>.txt (file CHINH). KEEP cap (Lotto 40, Mega 400) hoac P_SONG=0.3 (so cap lon nhat giu xac suat thanh cong >= 30%).
    chon_cap/bang_k_<game>_<ky>.txt = bang K -> xac suat thanh cong -> ve con lai.
    So du doan cap ghi truoc ky quay: chon_cap/du_doan_cap_<game>.jsonl.
 4. DOI CHIEU THAT: chon_cap/ket_qua_chon_cap_<game>.txt = moi ky da quay, bao nhieu cap da chon bi trung, ky do THANH CONG khong (ngoai mau).
 5. PHU: du_doan/<game>_<ky>.txt = ve con lai. CHI co nghia khi khong cap chon nao trung.

LUU Y TRUNG THUC: ky quay doc lap nen cap trung ky D khong phu thuoc cac ky truoc; so seed moi cap gan nhau (chenh ~0.4% Lotto, ~1% Mega).
Neu backtest khong quy tac nao hon ngau nhien thi chon cap nao cung nhu cap nao; xac suat thanh cong chi phu thuoc so cap chon (K). Khong co "cach chon cap" thang duoc ngau nhien
tru khi nghien cuu chung minh. Muon xac suat thanh cong cao thi chon it cap (K nho) - doi lai ve con lai con nhieu.

CHAY TAY: python3 chon_cap_release.py lotto535|mega TAG_RELEASE [KY]      (can gh CLI + GH_TOKEN)
          python3 nghien_cuu_chon_cap.py lotto535|mega                     (chi nghien cuu tren lich su da co, khong tai)
env: KEEP, P_SONG, TARGET, HIST, BATCH (tai song song, mac dinh 6), FORCE=1 (chay lai ky da co), WARM/ZPASS/ZTRAIN/PRIMARY (nghien cuu).
